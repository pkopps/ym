## Findings

### Home search ignores a typed address or ZIP and loops to a location prompt
**bug**, high
- Steps to repeat: open `/`, replace Where with "12 Bay Ave, Barnegat, NJ 08005" (or just "08005"), click "Find my match" or press Enter, wait a few seconds, then choose "Enter my area instead".
- Expected: matches for my address, or a clear message such as "Pick your town from the list".
- What happened: the "Find care near you" geolocation dialog opens. "Enter my area instead" sends me back to the same field, which is a loop. Search only works after picking an item from the suggestion list, and picking it throws away the street address. Someone on a phone who types their address will likely give up here.
- Screenshots: `01-home-1440x900.png`, `01-home-390x844.png` (start state). The dialog itself was seen in the browser pane only, because the capture tool cannot reproduce typed input.

### Filled-in booking dialog closed and reset without booking
**bug**, high
- Steps to repeat: on the matches page, click "See other dates", pick a next-week day, type a street, click "Book", answer the three property questions, and click "Book" again.
- Expected: go to "Review and confirm".
- What happened: on the first try the dialog closed and I was back on the list with no message. Reopening it showed every field reset. The second try with the same input worked. The console showed a dev Fast Refresh rebuild at about that time, so this may be a dev-server artifact, but nothing told me what happened or saved my input.
- Screenshots: `02-matches-1440x900.png`, `02-matches-390x844.png`

**bug**, medium (trust)

### Payment wording contradicts itself across screens
**friction**, medium (trust)
- Steps to repeat: compare the review page, the confirmation page, the dashboard and the notification.
- Expected: one consistent statement of when and how I pay the provider, for example after the visit, in cash or by card.
- What happened:
  - Review: "You pay Bot Lawn 0 directly. YardMatch doesn't charge you."
  - Confirmation: "will confirm before any payment is due."
  - Dashboard: "You pay after they confirm" and PAYMENT "After provider confirms".
  - Notification: "will confirm before payment is collected."

  "Collected" suggests YardMatch takes the money. "After they confirm" suggests I pay before the mowing happens. None of these says how I pay.
- Screenshots: browser pane only (signed-in pages).
