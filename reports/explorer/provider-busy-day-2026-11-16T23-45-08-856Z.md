# Explorer: provider on a busy day

- **Persona:** owner of Bot Lawn 0 in Barnegat, the evening before a day with 7 visits and 3 crew (auto-accept staffing, crew may lead routes).
- **Goal:** know every stop, answer anything waiting, and make sure the crew knows where to be.
- **Reached it:** mostly. I saw all seven stops with crew counts, confirmed the waiting request, and assigned a route leader, who was notified. Along the way a misplaced click started tomorrow's 9am route the night before, and the customer was told the crew was on the way.
- **Steps:** 14.
- **Run folder:** `reports/explorer/runs/provider-busy-day-2026-11-16T23-45-08-856Z/`

## Steps taken
1. Logged in; landed on "Upcoming jobs and customer requests." with a "Today path" card for Owner 700 (Requested) and four steps: Confirm, Crew, Route, Finish.
2. Read the page: Jobs, Business setup, Service prices, Card for finder fees, Service openings (18 rows), Business credentials, then the seven job cards, all on one page.
3. Each job card: schedule ("Morning • 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM" plus "Nov 17, 2026, 8:00 AM"), address, staffing (needed, assigned, open), status, route leader, and a big "Start route" button.
4. Clicked "Confirm booking" on Owner 700. No confirmation or fee notice; the card switched to Confirmed after a moment.
5. Opened "Card for finder fees": $75, $110, $150 by lawn size; "Your first new customer is free."; "No card saved yet."; "Card setup isn't available. Try again later." (no Stripe locally).
6. Opened "Assign a route leader" on the 8am job: a heading and one sentence, no controls.
7. Found "Staffing • 2 matches" under it and expanded it: crew cards with "Lead this route", "Call crew" and "Get help".
8. Clicked "Lead this route". The staffing list had collapsed as the page refreshed, so the click landed on the next job's "Start route" (Owner 701, 9am).
9. The API accepted it: Owner 701 is "On route" at 6:46pm the day before, and the customer got "Your crew is on the way".
10. Expanded the staffing list again and clicked "Lead this route" for Crew 801: "Route leader assigned."; Crew 801 got "You were assigned as route leader".
11. Looked for a way to stop the 9am route: none. The card offers before photos, "Start work", "Update customer about timing", customer chat and "Track crew".
12. Checked the stops: seven one-hour visits from 8am to 2pm across Barnegat, Manahawkin and Waretown, back to back.
13. Compared route leaders: the cards say "Route leader: Crew 800" or "Crew 801" on jobs where no leader was assigned (database empty).
14. Captured the page at both sizes.

## Findings

### 1. "Start route" works the night before and can't be undone (bug, high)
- **Repeat:** as a provider with a confirmed visit tomorrow, press "Start route" tonight.
- **Expected:** blocked until near the visit window, or at least "Start the route for tomorrow's 9am visit now?"; and a way to stop a route started by mistake.
- **Happened:** the route started 14 hours early, the customer was told "Your crew is on the way", and the card has no "Stop route". The bot run found the same rule from the API side: on-route, start and complete all work days before the scheduled start.
- **Screenshot:** `02-after-actions-1440x900.png`

### 2. The page re-renders and collapses what you opened, moving buttons under the cursor (bug, medium)
- **Repeat:** expand "Staffing • 2 matches" on a job and wait a few seconds before clicking.
- **Happened:** the list collapsed and the job below moved up; my click meant for "Lead this route" hit the next job's "Start route". With "Start route" the largest button on every card, that is a costly slip.

### 3. "Assign a route leader" has no controls (friction, medium)
- **Happened:** the box only says "Pick who starts and shares the route…". The buttons are under a separate collapsed "Staffing • N matches" link below it.

### 4. Cards show a route leader that was never assigned (gap, medium: screens disagree)
- **Happened:** "Route leader: Crew 800" on jobs whose route leader is empty in the database (the provider lets accepted crew lead by fallback). The provider can't tell an assigned leader from a guess, and the crew member may not know they are expected to lead.

### 5. Back-to-back visits across towns with no travel time (gap, high: provider economics)
- **Happened:** seven one-hour visits at 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 1 and 2 across Barnegat, Manahawkin (about 6 miles) and Waretown. Nothing allows for drive time, and two-person jobs share the same three crew.

### 6. Confirming a new customer says nothing about the finder fee (gap, medium)
- **Repeat:** "Confirm booking" on a request from a new customer.
- **Happened:** no mention that the first completed job with this customer triggers a $75/$110/$150 finder fee (or that it's the free first one). The fee terms are only in "Card for finder fees" further down the page.

### 7. New customers can be accepted with no card on file (gap, medium)
- **Happened:** "No card saved yet." but confirming new customers is allowed. The fee would fail when billing runs, after the work is done.

### 8. "Today path" shows tomorrow (friction, low)
- **Happened:** the top card says "Today path" for tomorrow's first stop.

### 9. One long page for everything (friction, medium)
- **Happened:** settings, fee card, 18 opening rows and credential forms sit above the seven job cards. On a phone you scroll past setup to reach the day's work.
- **Screenshot:** `01-management-390x844.png`

### 10. Window and time disagree (friction, low)
- **Happened:** "Morning • 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM" and "Nov 17, 2026, 8:00 AM" on the same card; openings are listed as "11/17/2026, 8:00:00 AM ET" with seconds and no end time.

## Places I had to guess

- Whether pressing "Start route" tonight would notify anyone (it did).
