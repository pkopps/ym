## What held
- One finder fee per owner–provider pair, at the right tier, with the first relationship waived. No billable fee on a disputed or cancelled first job after fast-forwarding billing past the 72-hour window. One fee per recurring plan.
- Live location refused to stranger owners and crew (650 of 650 probes).
- Password reset: unknown emails don't reveal an account; expired and used codes are refused; sessions from before the reset end; the old password stops working.
- Launch flags: crew marketplace and payout routes answer 404.
- Duplicate email and lockout after repeated wrong passwords.
- Search fill holds from about 15 providers in the simulated Barnegat market.

## Not covered in this pass
- 2FA, Google and Apple sign-in, and email or SMS content (no vendors locally).
- Provider decline and recovery in the browser, and crew GPS sharing in the browser (the bots cover them over HTTP).
- Real drive times: the simulation and the matcher use straight-line distance, so Long Beach Island looks closer than it is.
