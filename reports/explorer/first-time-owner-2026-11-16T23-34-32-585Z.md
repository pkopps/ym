# Explorer: first-time owner

- **Persona:** first-time property owner in Barnegat, NJ (08005), on a phone half the time.
- **Goal:** get the lawn mowed next week by someone trustworthy, and know the cost and who is coming.
- **Reached it:** partly. A booking request for Tue, Nov 24 (morning, Bot Lawn 0, $80.12) was sent and a confirmation page shown. "Manage my booking" then failed to load in this run because the web app and API had different launch flags (fixed in the runner; see finding 9).
- **Steps:** 24.
- **Run folder:** `reports/explorer/runs/first-time-owner-2026-11-16T23-34-32-585Z/` (brief, seed, API and web logs, screenshots).

## Findings

### 1. Typed street address is ignored by search (friction, high)
- **Repeat:** home page, type a full street address in "Where", click "Find my match".
- **Expected:** a search for that address.
- **Happened:** a "use your location" dialog. Only a picked town suggestion counts, and street-level addresses are never suggested. On a phone this looks like the button does nothing useful.
- **Screenshots:** `01-home-1440x900.png`, `01-home-390x844.png`

### 2. The "Where" field appends instead of replacing (friction, medium)
- **Repeat:** home page; click "Where" (shows "Barnegat, NJ") and type. Same on results with "Change service or area".
- **Expected:** the prefilled area is selected or cleared when you type.
- **Happened:** "Barnegat, NJ" is a real value, not a placeholder, so typing is appended ("Barnegat, NJ145 West Bay Ave"). (Select-all did not replace it in this run either, but select-all also failed in other forms with this browser tool, so that part is not counted.)

### 3. Far-away towns offered as "nearby" (friction, medium)
- **Repeat:** type in "Where" with Barnegat as the area.
- **Happened:** Hoboken, Jersey City and Newark appear as "Nearby local service-area guess", 60 to 70 miles away. Picking one gives "No providers are available in this area yet".

### 4. "★ 5 stars • 0 reviews" on new providers (gap, high: trust)
- **Repeat:** search lawn mowing in Barnegat.
- **Expected:** no rating, or "New", until there are reviews.
- **Happened:** every new provider shows five stars with zero reviews, which reads as invented.
- **Screenshot:** `02-matches-1440x900.png`

### 5. No price on the match cards (gap, medium)
- **Repeat:** search results.
- **Happened:** the price first appears after picking a date, typing the address, answering three questions and pressing Book. The goal "know what it will cost" cannot be met while choosing between providers.

### 6. "See other dates" opens off-screen (friction, medium)
- **Repeat:** results page, "See other dates" on the first card.
- **Happened:** the scheduling dialog opens below the fold with no scroll or focus change; it looks like nothing happened.

### 7. Address asked three times (friction, medium)
- **Happened:** typed on the home page, again in the scheduling dialog (the street was empty), and again on the sign-up form, even though the review page already showed it.

### 8. No cancellation terms or payment method before committing (gap, high)
- **Repeat:** reach "Review and confirm".
- **Expected:** what cancelling costs and until when; how and to whom you pay (at launch, customers pay the provider directly).
- **Happened:** only "No charge now. Your provider confirms before payment." There's no cancellation policy, no mention of paying the provider directly, and no link beyond the footer's "Refunds".
- **Screenshot:** the browser pane only. The capture tool cannot show this screen because the draft lives in the original tab (see finding 11).

### 9. Customer dashboard failed: "Route not found" (environment, not counted)
- **Repeat:** book as a new customer, then "Manage my booking".
- **Happened:** "Welcome back, there." and "We couldn't load your account: Route not found (Reference …)". The dashboard calls `GET /api/payments`, which the API answers 404 when payments are off.
- **Note:** in this run the web app had payments on (local default) and the API had them off. The runner now gives both the launch configuration. With both off (owner-two-properties run) the dashboard loads, so this is not a launch bug. Still worth noting: one section's 404 blanks the whole account page, and the greeting falls back to "there".

### 10. "Morning" is not a time (friction, low)
- **Happened:** booking, review and confirmation all say "Tue, Nov 24 · Morning" with no window. Evening was offered for a provider whose openings are 8am to 5pm.

### 11. A review link opened elsewhere says the details "expired" (friction, low)
- **Repeat:** copy the review URL to another tab or device.
- **Happened:** "Your booking details expired. Choose a provider and opening again." The draft is per tab, not expired.
- **Screenshot:** `03-review-1440x900.png`

### 12. Price shown to the cent (friction, low)
- **Happened:** "$80.12" reads as a computed number rather than a quote a local business would give.

### 13. Mobile header overflows (bug, low)
- **Repeat:** any page at 390x844.
- **Happened:** the language picker is cut off at the right edge of the second header row.
- **Screenshot:** `02-matches-390x844.png`
