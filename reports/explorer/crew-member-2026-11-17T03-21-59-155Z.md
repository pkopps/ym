(b) Nothing tells me what to do on site: no notes, gate or access info, property details, checklist or photo requirements.
- **Steps:** about 30 actions over about 12 minutes.
- **Run folder / screenshots:** `/Users/patrickkopps/Documents/app/reports/explorer/runs/crew-member-2026-11-17T03-21-59-155Z/`

## Findings

### 1. Jobs for Wednesday sit under "Today", and nothing says "Tomorrow"
**bug, high**

- **Steps to repeat:** Log in as the crew account in the evening ET (I was on Mon Nov 16 at 22:22 ET, which is Nov 17 03:22 UTC). Open /crew.
- **Expected:** "Today" shows today's work, or is empty if there is none. Upcoming days carry a weekday and a relative label ("Tomorrow, Tue Nov 17"), so I can answer "where am I tomorrow?" at a glance.
- **What happened:** The sub-tab is "Today", but every card under it is dated "Nov 18, 2026" with no weekday. Only the Schedule page adds the weekday ("Wed, Nov 18"), and that is two days away in my time zone. I can't tell whether I have nothing tomorrow, or the dates are shifted (the time is close to the UTC day boundary). Either way, the main question isn't answered clearly. The "New device signed in" update also mixes zones: the body says "11/17/2026, 3:22:38 AM UTC" and the timestamp says "Nov 16, 10:22 PM".
- **Screenshots:** `02-crew-jobs-1440x900.png`, `02-crew-jobs-390x844.png`, `03-schedule-1440x900.png`

### 2. Map links leave out town, state and ZIP
**bug, high**

- **Steps to repeat:** /crew/schedule, then look at the Google Maps or Apple Maps link on any stop.
- **Expected:** Navigation goes to "816 Oak Ln, Barnegat, NJ 08005".
- **What happened:** The links are `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=816%20Oak%20Ln` and `https://maps.apple.com/?q=807%20Route%209`. "807 Route 9" or "289 Gunning River Rd" with no town can easily resolve to the wrong place. That means a crew driving to the wrong address and a missed visit.
- **Screenshots:** `03-schedule-1440x900.png`, `03-schedule-390x844.png`

### 3. Nothing tells me what to do when I get there

- **What happened:** Each card only says "lawn mowing", the hours, the address and "Bot Lawn 0 accepted you for this job." The cards don't open a detail view, and notifications don't link to a job.

## What a crew member can or cannot see

- **Can do:** see assigned jobs and pay rate ($22/hr), start the route (only from 3 hours before), call the business (`tel:555-0200`), open a support ticket, cancel a single assignment (with a native confirm), and change service, day and radius preferences.
- **Cannot do:** call off a whole day, add photos (not visible before the route starts), see job notes, or open a job detail page.
- **Things a crew member should not see:** I found nothing sensitive. /ops asks for an ops login. /provider is the public sales page with no business data. No customer names, phone numbers or prices were shown on the crew screens. Small issue: the /ops blocked screen copy narrates implementation ("now requires an authorized ops session instead of loading anonymously").
