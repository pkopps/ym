## Check before launch

- The login page shows "Local reset code: NNNNNN" for real accounts and not for unknown emails. This is fine locally, but confirm it can never render outside local mode, or it skips email and reveals which emails have accounts.
- Google and Apple sign-in links on port 3012 point to localhost:3000. This is probably the explorer's port setup (`APP_BASE_URL` for OAuth), not the product.
- Card setup for finder fees says "isn't available" because no payment provider is configured locally. That's expected here, but the message gives no next step.

## Explorer tooling notes

- The capture tool signs in fresh each time, so every capture adds a "New device signed in" notification, and some personas' captures show signed-out pages. Several findings were seen only in the browser pane and have no saved screenshot.
- The provider-busy-day run sent one real (local) delay notice to Owner 700 by testing the button.
