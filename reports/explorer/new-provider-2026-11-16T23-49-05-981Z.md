# Explorer: new provider onboarding

- **Persona:** owner of a two-person lawn business in Waretown, NJ, with general liability insurance, referred by a friend.
- **Goal:** sign up and get to where homeowners can book me.
- **Reached it:** partly. The account was created and is "pending review". Credentials were not submitted: the form sits in a collapsed section, and it asks only for a reference number. Bookable status depends on ops approval, with no time given.
- **Steps:** 12.
- **Run folder:** `reports/explorer/runs/new-provider-2026-11-16T23-49-05-981Z/`

## Steps taken
1. From the site header, opened "For providers": "Get lawn care customers near your routes." with what you get and pricing ($75, $110, $150 finder fee; first customer free; no per-job fee; optional Pro $49/month and Teams $99/month).
2. Clicked "Sign up your business": one long form with name, business name, email, phone, password twice, business address, service radius (15), 13 service checkboxes (Lawn mowing and Seasonal cleanup pre-checked), and Service prices.
3. Filled it in. Email and phone are plain text fields.
4. Service prices: "Custom pricing isn't available for this service and ZIP yet." for both services in 08758 (see note).
5. The password rule appears at the bottom of the form, above "Create account".
6. Clicked "Create account": landed on the management page, "No scheduled jobs."
7. Business setup: "Your business is pending review before it appears in search." Status pending review, submitted time.
8. "Next steps": update years of experience, "Be ready to handle evidence requests, service recovery, and customer communication at the launch service standard", submit credentials "before public launch visibility".
9. Verification checklist with Ready, Review and Needs update rows, written for ops ("ops should confirm…", "A 15-mile radius is still tight enough for a dense launch route", "counsel-approved exemption").
10. Looked for where to submit insurance: "Business credentials" under "Settings and calendar", collapsed. It has a type, a reference and an expiry date, with no file upload.
11. Looked for how long approval takes: nothing.
12. Captured the landing page and the pending-review page at both sizes.

## Findings

### 1. Internal ops and launch language on a provider screen (bug, high; production-content rules)
- **Repeat:** sign up as a provider; read "Next steps" and "Verification checklist".
- **Happened:** "at the launch service standard", "before public launch visibility", "still tight enough for a dense launch route", "ops should confirm service reliability expectations directly", "Intervention readiness", "counsel-approved exemption". This is the internal review rubric shown to the applicant, which the AGENTS.md content rules forbid.
- **Screenshot:** `02-pending-review-1440x900.png`

### 2. No way to upload proof of insurance or registration (gap, high)
- **Happened:** a credential is a type, a free-text reference and an expiry date. Nothing tells the provider what ops will check, or how a certificate of insurance gets to YardMatch.

### 3. No estimate of approval time and no "we'll email you" (gap, medium)
- **Happened:** "pending review" with a submitted time, but no expected wait and no word on how you'll hear back.

### 4. The credential form is hidden from the steps that ask for it (friction, medium)
- **Happened:** "Next steps" says to submit credentials, but the form is in a collapsed "Settings and calendar" area further down, with no link from the step.

### 5. Years of experience assumed, never asked (bug, medium)
- **Happened:** sign-up has no experience or description fields, but the checklist says "1 year of experience may be acceptable", and "Next" asks me to update it.

### 6. Pricing step says custom pricing isn't available (friction, medium; possibly environment)
- **Happened:** "Custom pricing isn't available for this service and ZIP yet." for 08758 on both services, with no starting price shown. The explorer database has no ZIP minimums seeded, so this may not happen on a seeded environment. If it can happen for a real launch ZIP, the provider has no idea what customers will be quoted.

### 7. Plans listed without what they include (gap, low)
- **Happened:** Pro ($49/month) and Teams ($99/month) are named on the landing page and the sign-up form, with only "never change who YardMatch recommends".

### 8. Form details (friction, low)
- Email and phone are `type="text"`, so phones don't get the right keyboard.
- The password rule appears at the bottom, not next to the password fields.
- The ZIP field accepts 15 digits.
- The service radius has no unit.
- Lawn mowing and Seasonal cleanup are pre-checked.

## Worked well
- The finder-fee pricing is stated plainly and the same way on the landing page, the sign-up form and the fee card.
