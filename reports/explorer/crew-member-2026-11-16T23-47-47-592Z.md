## Findings

### 1. No arrival instructions (gap, high)
- **Repeat:** crew jobs page, any job.
- **Expected:** what the job includes (service details, the customer's property notes and gate code, where to park, any photos needed), and who leads.
- **Happened:** only provider, service name, time, address and hourly rate. The crew can't prepare without calling the business.
- **Screenshot:** `01-crew-jobs-1440x900.png`

### 2. Crew can start a route the night before (bug, high)
- **Repeat:** a crew member who leads a route (10am and 2pm here) presses "Start route" the evening before.
- **Happened:** the button is live now, 14+ hours early. The provider run showed this is accepted and tells the customer "Your crew is on the way".

### 3. Back-to-back jobs in different towns (gap, medium)
- **Happened:** 12–1pm Manahawkin, then 1–2pm Waretown (about 9 miles), with no travel time; the crew sees the conflict before the business does.

### 4. "Today" tab for tomorrow's work (friction, low)
- **Happened:** all jobs are Nov 17; the page has a "Today" tab next to "Jobs", and it's unclear which shows tomorrow.

### 5. Job card on a request the business hasn't accepted (friction, low)
- **Happened:** the 8am job reads "ACCEPTED" (the crew was accepted) and also "WAITING ON BUSINESS CONFIRMATION" (the customer's request isn't confirmed). Two statuses on one card mean different things.

### 6. Pay shows a rate but not hours or total (friction, low)
- **Happened:** "$22/hr" on each job; five one-hour jobs with no daily total or estimate.

## Worked well
- Cancelling an assignment asks first and says what it costs the crew member.
- No customer phone number or other customers' data was visible.
