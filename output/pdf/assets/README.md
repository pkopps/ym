## Final image prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium realistic studio device illustration for a printed YardMatch landscaping partner brochure. Wide 3:2 composition, very high resolution, warm off-white seamless background #f7f5ef, subtle natural contact shadows, calm forest green #205938 and cream interfaces. Show exactly five physical devices: a large elegant silver desktop monitor in back, a landscape iPad tablet slightly in front on the right, and three distinct upright smartphones clearly separated across the front. All screens face the viewer almost straight on and are readable, realistic proportions, no hands, no props, no extra devices. Desktop shows YardMatch provider weekly schedule with tasteful calendar rows and small job cards; iPad shows provider route / job management with a few task rows and subtle map panel. Three front phones show three different YardMatch apps: left 'Customer' with 'Book lawn care', a small beautiful lawn photo and a green 'Find my match' button; center 'Provider' with 'Today’s jobs', three tidy job cards; right 'Crew' with 'My assignments', a job card and green 'View assignment' button. Brand text 'YardMatch' at top of each screen. Professional designed interfaces with large simple accurate headings and sparse supporting text, no gibberish dense tables. Emphasize polished tangible hardware and believable calm app screens. Tight balanced product group occupying most of image, generous clean margins, every device fully within frame. No store badges, QR codes, floating text labels, captions, headlines, watermarks, or Apple hardware logos outside the screens. This is a conceptual product visualization, not an exact screenshot.
