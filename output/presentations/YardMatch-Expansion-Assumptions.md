# YardMatch national expansion assumptions

Updated September 27, 2026. These are investor-planning assumptions, not operating results or a promised launch schedule.

The consolidated forecast is now national. Fourteen named New Jersey municipalities remain in the workbook as pilot evidence and a proxy for service and property mix; they no longer cap the company footprint.

## Base rollout

| Year | Active service areas | States | Metro clusters | New service areas |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2027 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2028 | 6 | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| 2029 | 25 | 2 | 3 | 19 |
| 2030 | 70 | 4 | 6 | 45 |
| 2031 | 175 | 10 | 14 | 105 |
| 2032 | 400 | 25 | 30 | 225 |

A service area may be one municipality, a group of neighboring small municipalities, or a suburban neighborhood cluster. The base plan becomes multi-state in 2029 and reaches a broad national footprint by 2033. The downside reaches 400 service areas in 25 states; the upside reaches 1,200 areas in 48 states.

## Selected revenue and payment model

- Finder fees are $75 typical, $110 large, and $150 very large, paid after the first completed paid job in a new customer/provider relationship.
- Each new provider receives one finder-fee waiver.
- Pro is $49 per business per month. Teams is $99 per business per month and includes Pro.
- Pro includes local crew recruiting and applicant management. Individual crew members pay $0.
- Customers pay providers directly using cash, check, or the provider’s existing payment method.
- YardMatch does not process homeowner service payments, issue provider payouts, or build a proprietary payment engine in the forecast.
- Projects and repeat visits with the same provider create no second finder fee and no recurring YardMatch service fee.

## Base cost assumptions

- Customer CAC improves from $55 in 2027 to $30 in 2033.
- Launch spend is $5,000 per new service area.
- Annual coverage is $2,000 per active service area.
- Regional operations are $20,000 per metro cluster.
- One active provider supports 40 year-end households.
- Provider annual retention is 85%.
- Paid staff scale from 3 in 2027 to 60 in 2033.
- Average fully loaded compensation rises from $100,000 to $110,000.
- The company is fully remote. Corporate overhead grows from $42,000 to $550,000 as the national organization expands.

The workbook includes only an illustrative $1.5m initial round in the cash balance; national expansion therefore requires staged follow-on financing.
