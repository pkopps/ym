# YardMatch revenue model

Updated September 27, 2026. Proposed prices and adoption are investor-planning assumptions.

## Selected model

YardMatch monetizes two provider outcomes:

1. **A new customer relationship:** $75 typical, $110 large, or $150 very large property, charged once after the first completed paid job. The first relationship is waived for each new provider.
2. **Software that helps the provider absorb demand:** Pro at $49 per business per month and Teams at $99 per business per month, including Pro.

Customers pay providers directly. YardMatch charges no recurring percentage or flat fee on service work, handles no homeowner service funds, and issues no provider payouts.

## Provider plans

| Plan | Price | Included value |
|---|---:|---|
| Core | No subscription | Marketplace access, booking, job details, crew assignments, and essential communication |
| Pro | $49/month | Crew recruiting, applicant management, scheduling, customer history, estimates, routes, and reporting |
| Teams | $99/month including Pro | Multi-crew permissions, labor costing, and utilization |

Pro and Teams are mutually exclusive tiers. Crew members pay nothing. Subscription status does not improve the provider’s customer-match ranking.

The base model assumes Pro-only adoption reaches 40% of average active providers and Teams reaches 20% by 2033. Net subscription revenue is $3.1m in 2033 after credits; related cost of revenue is $0.5m and incremental subscription operating expense is $0.1m.

## Sponsored recommendations

Sponsorship remains outside the selected forecast. A later pilot could offer one clearly labeled sponsored backup among providers that already pass fit, capacity, verification, and reliability thresholds. The main recommendation would remain quality-first.

The current workbook contains an optional sponsorship case for testing, with no assumed increase in customers, retention, or finder revenue. It should not launch until the marketplace has enough qualified supply and demand for a randomized holdout.

## Revenue outcome

| 2033 base | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Finder-fee revenue | $21.1m |
| Net Pro and Teams revenue | $3.1m |
| Total YardMatch revenue | $24.2m |
| Provider gross booking value | $653.8m |
| YardMatch revenue / gross booking value | 3.7% |

The low share of gross booking value reflects the decision to protect provider margins. The model depends on continuing new-market acquisition and subscription adoption rather than taking a percentage of repeat service revenue.
