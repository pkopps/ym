## Current behavior and limits

Credential controls use the existing credential reference contract. They do not introduce public document uploads: references must point to approved secure records, and only ops can review them. Existing category requirements and provider responsibility remain authoritative. New providers can publish availability before approval, but public discovery and booking still require current verified credentials and approved visibility.

Recovery is persisted as booking JSON. Decline/withdrawal preserves the request until the customer consents. Recovery reads can open overdue timing recovery; the existing scheduler continues its overdue escalation. Alternatives are capped at three and must have bookable openings, matching categories, radius, current credentials and the same recalculated total. Legacy or changed scope/prices require support. Customer recovery does not bypass payment, proof, capability or refund gates.

Recurring plan consent changes subsequent generation; already generated sibling visits are deliberately refused for individual review. Ordinary customer timing limits remain distinct from provider-caused recovery. Once travel/work starts, self-service timing/property/recovery actions cannot silently move the job. Support handles access failures, broader scope corrections and unresolved payment outcomes.

The provider and crew remain responsible for engaging, supervising, insuring and compensating crew. No YardMatch crew wage or crew transfer was added. Referral incentives, partnerships, advanced assistant commands, deeper calendar integration and richer performance reporting remain outside this change.
